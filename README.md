# whatIsInTheFridge
What's in the Cupboard? is a collaborative inventory management web application designed for households to track their supplies, reduce waste, and manage consumption efficiently



This application allows a group of people with a shared interest in home organization and food management to catalog their "things"—from groceries to household essentials. Users can maintain multiple collections (e.g., Fridge, Pantry, Cleaning Supplies) and track specific details for every item.🛠 Key FeaturesCollection Management: Users can create and manage multiple collections of items based on their location in the home.Inventory Tracking: Add, edit, and delete items including their names, descriptions, and quantities.Weight & Volume Updates: Functionality to update specific item metrics like weight (grams) or quantity.Expiry Date Monitoring: Track the shelf-life of items to ensure timely consumption.
